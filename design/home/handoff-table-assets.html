  1. An NGO posts a task it can describe in a sentence.

    Verified organizations carry a seal, checked against public nonprofit records.

  2. You claim it in its time lane.

    Every task fits 15, 20, or 30 minutes, so you pick by the time you have.

  3. You do the work, right now.

    No onboarding call, no shift. The clock on the task is the whole commitment.

  4. You hand back a link to what you did.

    Proof stays on the platform, so the NGO reviews it where the task lives.

  5. The NGO approves it.

    Only the organization that posted the task can approve it.

  6. Approval mints your badge.

    Badges are earned on approval, never on claim.
