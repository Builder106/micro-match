# MicroMatch Roadmap

High-level roadmap and milestone goals for MicroMatch, ordered by launch priority.

## v1.0 — MVP Community Launch (Fall 2026)

- **Wesleyan Community Launch**: Campus onboarding for orientation and student-org fairs.
- **Single-Player Badge Vault**: Allow volunteers to log off-platform service hours and verify via organization email contact.
- **Concierge Task Seeding**: 20–30 initial micro-tasks curated across campus groups, professors, and open-source civic initiatives (Wikipedia, OpenStreetMap, Project Gutenberg).
- **Core Verification & Claim Loop**: Seamless claim submission, lightweight proof-of-work review, and automated badge awarding.

## v1.1 — Gamification & Org Telemetry

- **XP & Level Progression**: Persisted XP points and progress milestones in Appwrite.
- **NGO Analytics Dashboard**: Aggregated volunteer hours, completion rates, and active task stats for registered nonprofits.
- **Leaderboards & Streaks**: Opt-in privacy-preserving volunteer streaks and community impact leaderboards.

## v1.2 — Multi-Language & Real-time Collab

- **Automated Multi-lingual Task Delivery**: Expanded server-side translations for task briefs and guidelines via self-hosted translation endpoints.
- **Reviewer Messaging**: Integrated lightweight messaging channel for NGO task leads to clarify submission proofs directly with volunteers.
- **PWA & Offline Task Caching**: Installable Progressive Web App with offline task browsing and draft proof caching.

## Out of Scope

Per [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and founder student visa constraints:

- Paid commercial transactions, monetization, or fee-taking
- Complex employment / contractor payroll features
- Direct advertising networks or intrusive data tracking
