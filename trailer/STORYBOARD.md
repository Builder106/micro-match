# Tier 3 trailer storyboard

Built in HyperFrames. `MicroMatchTrailer` is 45 seconds at 1920×1080, 30 fps.
The first fifteen seconds are the idea with no product in them: what
volunteering usually asks for, what people have, and what nonprofits have
waiting. The site only comes in once both sides are on the table.

| Time | Beat | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.00-9.50 | "Most volunteering asks for a Saturday morning. Or a semester." Then "Plenty of people have fifteen minutes," with fifteen running stitches, one a minute | `compositions/open.html` |
| 9.50-15.50 | "Plenty of nonprofits have jobs that size waiting." Three seed tasks as pinked swatches, gathered toward the middle | `compositions/pieces.html` |
| 15.50-24.50 | The site loads and the quilt settles: "MicroMatch puts the two together." | `entrance` take, 0.00 s |
| 24.50-29.00 | A block unfolds into "How a patch is made": "Every block of the quilt opens." | `unfold` take, 1.00 s |
| 29.00-35.00 | An approved claim is sewn into the community block: "A person checks every task, then it's sewn in." | `sewn` take, 0.00 s |
| 35.00-39.50 | The quilt in Arabic, right to left, then in dark mode: "In seven languages, by lamplight too." | `languages` take, 5.80 s |
| 39.50-45.00 | End card: the sawtooth border pieces in around "Small tasks, sewn into something warm." and trymicromatch.com | `compositions/end.html` |

Captions are in `compositions/captions.html`. They sit bottom left as signed
muslin squares, like the labels on the quilt's blocks, and move to the right
for the Arabic shot so they don't cover the copy.

Sound: `audio.mp3` fades in over the first 1.2 s and out over the last 1.8 s.
On top of that, one sew at 29.5 s as the patch goes in. Credit the music the
way `public/CREDITS.md` says when you publish.
